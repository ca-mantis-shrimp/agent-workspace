# Plural agency

Determine whether a durable plural agent collective can improve consequential
planning and continuity relative to one capable agent using Git and a short
handoff, without persistent council machinery, centralized planning authority,
activity surveillance, or increased human continuity burden.

The governing design and its independent review are recorded in
[Networked agency and the continuity commons](../../knowledge/design/networked-agency-continuity-commons.md)
and the
[council review](../../knowledge/evaluations/networked-agency-council-review.md).
They are hypotheses to falsify, not implementation requirements.

## Product thesis

The durable subject is the collective. Individual model invocations, harness
sessions, machines, and worktrees are temporary runtimes or platforms through
which portions of it reason and act. A participant joins by inheriting a bounded
public state, works locally, deliberately publishes a consequential delta, and
may then disappear without taking the collective's continuity with it.

This does not imply a hive mind or complete cognitive transparency. Local
exploration remains local. The commons retains accepted commitments, evidence,
dissent, uncertainty, and integrated results—not transcripts, heartbeats,
activity feeds, productivity signals, or private reasoning.

The collective is federated rather than supervisor-led:

- local plans and reversible work remain autonomous by default;
- shared agreement is required only at intersecting or authority-changing
  boundaries;
- the smallest actually affected group deliberates under subsidiarity;
- competing proposals and implementations remain first-class when convergence
  is unnecessary;
- evidence lineage and affected scope matter more than agent count;
- temporary councils are reconciliation operations, not privileged governing
  bodies;
- the human retains constitutional authority over ends, permissions, spending,
  credentials, publication, and consequential external effects.

The pronoun **we** is earned by accepted public commitments. One runtime's
proposal, or several correlated reports, must not be presented as collective
belief before adoption. Provenance and preserved disagreement prevent a
collective voice from laundering individual judgment.

## First evaluation

Use one real consequential maintenance fork under two isolated conditions with
the same mandate, evidence, absolute spending ceiling, and worker capability:

1. **Control:** one capable agent, Git, a short handoff document, and ordinary
   messaging.
2. **Treatment:** two independent read-only advisors, a per-run throwaway claim
   matrix, at most one cross-examination, and then the same ordinary tools.

Both arms receive equivalent misleading evidence and cold-successor conditions.
One wrong-but-current assertion is load-bearing and exposure is independently
verified. One treatment response is withheld at a predeclared point to test
degraded operation; silence remains unresolved and never becomes assent.

Score verified conclusions, continuation correctness, independently confirmed
material findings, preserved dissent, human recap burden, publication effort,
elapsed time, and total attributable cost. Prefer the simpler baseline when the
treatment does not improve verified outcomes or continuity burden enough to
justify its additional cost.

## Required properties

1. The brief, evidence set, exclusions, budgets, timeout, challenge rules, and
   stopping conditions are fixed before either arm runs.
2. A facilitator exposes synthesis but has no acceptance authority and cannot
   silently decide material brief challenges.
3. Deterministic checks outrank consensus on deterministic questions.
4. Repeated claims sharing a source are correlated reports, not independent
   evidence, and agent-count voting is never used.
5. Missing participation cannot authorize an irreversible or
   authority-expanding transition; only the affected boundary pauses.
6. Publication is an explicit reviewed act. Local commits and harness capture do
   not automatically enter the commons.
7. The trial records recommendations, evidence, dissent, uncertainty, reopening
   conditions, context modes, costs, failures, and owner disposition—not private
   reasoning or behavioral traces.
8. No result may silently reopen coordination entities cancelled by the existing
   selection disposition.
9. Clearhead may record durable commitments in this repository but is neither a
   required runtime dependency nor evidence that protocol integration is needed.
10. A negative result, honest impossibility, or stop within budget is a valid and
    publishable outcome.

## Authority boundaries

- The owner sets the mandate, consequential boundaries, spending ceiling,
  publication set, and which experimental result may affect real work.
- Git owns repository bytes, revisions, branches, worktrees, diffs, and whether
  an integration landed.
- Deterministic tools own their native checks.
- Repository knowledge records accepted rationale and evidence with provenance.
- Clearhead records this project's commitments, priorities, predecessors, and
  lifecycle; it does not represent executions, presence, votes, or private
  decomposition.
- Agent Workspace owns situated epistemic continuity and freshness, not the
  collective mandate or canonical documents.
- Harnesses launch and connect runtimes but gain no governing authority from
  doing so.

## Walking-skeleton scenario

A real maintenance decision with more than one defensible path is selected. The
owner resolves the remaining authority and budget choices. A predeclared control
and treatment run in isolation. A cold successor in each arm receives only the
allowed durable artifacts and must continue without a human recap. Independent
checks score outcomes and verify exposure to the planted misleading assertion.
The final report preserves dissent and failures, compares against the simpler
baseline, and recommends keep, change, or reject.

The first run is a falsification screen. At most two replications may be proposed
only if it is promising. Worker-tier economics are a separate future experiment
and are not authorized by this charter until council value survives.

## Non-goals

- Building a council platform, daemon, schema, reusable claim-matrix service,
  presence system, voting system, reputation system, or global planner.
- Integrating council proposals or execution attempts into Clearhead before
  repeated friction demonstrates a need.
- Autonomous spending, permission expansion, publication, merges, or external
  effects.
- Requiring collective ratification for ordinary investigation, bounded
  implementation, deterministic checks, or reversible local choices.
- Establishing that agents possess a literal shared consciousness or that the
  system is fully decentralized despite centralized compute and credentials.
- Testing whether strong deliberation enables cheaper workers before the
  council-value comparison succeeds.

## Completion criteria

This charter completes when one predeclared matched trial has run and its
keep/change/reject decision is published with evidence, failures, costs, dissent,
and owner disposition. Up to two additional matched trials may be added only if
the first is promising. The closing decision must state whether ordinary Git,
short handoffs, repository knowledge, and the existing workspace substrate are
sufficient; whether any protocol refinement deserves another test; and whether
concrete repeated friction justifies proposing machinery.

A finding that the plural-agency treatment adds ceremony or cost without better
verified outcomes or lower human continuity burden is successful completion, not
a reason to enlarge the system.
