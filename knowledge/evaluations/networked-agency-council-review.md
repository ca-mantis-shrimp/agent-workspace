# Council review — networked agency and the continuity commons

**Date:** 2026-09-15  
**Question:** Does the networked-agency and continuity-commons proposal provide
continuity without hierarchy, bureaucracy, or pervasive observability, and
what smallest falsifiable experiment should test it against simpler tools?

## Recommendation

Keep the proposal's governing rules and run a bounded falsification trial, but
build no council infrastructure. In particular:

- remember consequences, not behavior;
- treat a council as a temporary event around a consequential plan question;
- let a facilitator expose synthesis without granting it acceptance authority;
- keep durable actions distinct from execution attempts;
- keep Clearhead optional and defer integration;
- treat consensus, freshness, and repeated reports as neither truth nor
  independent evidence;
- test council value before testing whether expensive council judgment enables
  cheaper workers.

Both advisors retained this recommendation after cross-examination. No disputed
claim remains that another advisor pass could settle from existing evidence;
the remaining choices belong to the owner or to a real experiment.

## Accepted refinements

### Brief authority

An immutable brief prevents invisible prompt drift but can concentrate agenda
power. Every advisor may challenge the question, omissions, evidence set, or
framing during the independent pass. Materiality should be predeclared as a
mandate conflict or omission that invalidates the comparison—not ordinary
disagreement.

A material challenge ends that council attempt and creates a new, versioned
brief. The new version records the challenge, its disposition, what changed,
the cited evidence set, and deliberate exclusions. The facilitator may not
silently determine materiality. A disputed classification goes to the owner,
with a small fixed rebrief limit so challenge rights cannot become an
indefinite veto.

### Publication membrane

For the trial, local capture and shared publication are distinct scopes, but no
claim is made that existing storage enforces confidentiality. Share only an
explicitly selected set of summaries, claim matrices, decisions, and dissent.
Do not publish transcripts, scratch work, credentials, private files, capture
stores, or behavioral traces.

A local Git commit is not necessarily publication; define publication as push
or other external sharing. Review the selected diff before publication. State
plainly that external session stores, runner logs, and harness histories exist
outside the proposed membrane. Publication follows consequential outcomes,
never a timed cadence from which activity or productivity might be inferred.

### Timeout and missing participation

Silence is not assent. Authority-expanding or irreversible choices default to
no change. If whether an effect is reversible, disjoint, affected, or within
mandate is itself disputed, treat that boundary as affected and irreversible
until the owner classifies it.

"No change" must not mean continuing a disputed harmful effect. Pause only the
affected boundary; unrelated reversible work may continue. A discriminating
experiment must already be authorized, reversible, inside the spending ceiling,
and unable to settle the disputed external effect merely by being run. Use one
fixed deadline with no heartbeat-like automatic polling, and record missing
participation as unresolved rather than agreement with the status quo.

### Experiment shape

Use one real consequential maintenance fork under two isolated conditions with
the same mandate, evidence, absolute spending ceiling, and worker capability:

- **Control:** one capable agent, Git, a short handoff document, and ordinary
  messaging.
- **Treatment:** two independent read-only council advisors, a per-run throwaway
  claim matrix, at most one cross-examination, then the same ordinary tools.

Give both arms equivalent misleading evidence and cold-successor conditions.
Make one wrong-but-current assertion load-bearing and independently verify
exposure and outcomes. Withhold one treatment response at a predeclared point;
report that this tests degraded operation, especially when only two advisors
exist, rather than ordinary council value.

Score independently where feasible:

- accepted conclusions against external checks;
- continuation correctness after replacement;
- unique material issues confirmed against sources;
- preservation of dissent;
- human recap burden;
- publication effort;
- elapsed time;
- total attributable advisor, synthesis, successor, and verification cost.

Treat the first matched run as a falsification screen. Repeat up to three trials
only if promising. Prefer the simpler baseline when verified outcomes and human
continuity burden do not improve while cost rises. An approximate two-times
control cost is a useful predeclared review or kill trigger, not a statistically
justified quality multiplier. Set an absolute ceiling because control cost is
unknown before execution.

Hard-stop for unauthorized effects, private-data publication, facilitator
mandate expansion, mandatory ceremony reaching ordinary fixes, or creation of
a persistent council entity, schema, daemon, or reusable claim-matrix service
before evidence earns it.

### Worker economics

Keep worker capability constant in the council-value trial. Only if council
value survives should a separate matched experiment compare worker tiers. That
later accounting must include council, escalation, integration, verification,
and rework costs; better planning cannot be assumed to compensate for weaker
execution.

## Rejected interpretations

- More advisors or stronger models automatically provide independent evidence.
- A bounded wake summary constitutes a privacy boundary.
- Automatic capture is equivalent to intentional publication.
- A facilitator may resolve disputed classifications through synthesis.
- Missing participation authorizes an experiment or plan transition.
- Council quality already demonstrates that cheaper workers are viable.
- The current proposal justifies Clearhead integration or new coordination
  entities.

## Owner decisions before a real trial

1. Choose the consequential maintenance question and control condition.
2. Decide whether brief author and facilitator must be different participants
   in the first trial.
3. Decide who judges brief-challenge materiality and the maximum brief versions.
4. Set the absolute spending ceiling and numeric cost review trigger.
5. Choose an independent planter for misleading evidence and an outcome scorer.
6. Decide which arm's result, if either, may affect real repository work.
7. Confirm the publication set and who reviews it before push or external
   sharing.
8. Name who independently checks the hard-stop conditions.

The owner has already decided to defer Clearhead integration until repeated use
shows a need.

## Confidence and what would change the decision

**Confidence: medium.** The advisors converged strongly on the governance and
subtraction principles, but the council has not yet been compared with a
simpler decision path on real consequential work. The advisors also shared the
parent's framing and partially shared model lineage, so agreement is correlated.

Keep the protocol if repeated matched trials surface independently verified
material issues or reduce human continuity burden at acceptable total cost
while preserving dissent. Prefer the baseline if treatment adds cost and
ceremony without those gains. Reconsider machinery only after a concrete,
repeated failure cannot be addressed with bounded Markdown artifacts, Git, and
existing tools.
