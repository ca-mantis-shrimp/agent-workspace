# Charter plural agency as a falsifiable research program

**Status:** accepted by the owner on 2026-09-15 and applied to Clearhead.

## Decision

Create the `plural-agency` child charter as a research program, not an
implementation program. Its thesis treats the durable agent subject as a plural
collective instantiated through temporary runtimes while preserving local
privacy, scoped commitments, subsidiarity, evidence lineage, dissent,
forkability, and the owner's constitutional authority.

Create only two actions:

1. `plural-agency-trial-contract`, priority 2, staged behind
   `knowledge-pulse-dogfood`, to select a real consequential fork and predeclare
   the comparison after the owner resolves the remaining authority and budget
   choices.
2. `plural-agency-first-trial`, priority 2, staged behind the protocol, to run one
   matched falsification screen against a capable single-agent Git-and-handoff
   baseline.

Do not create implementation, Clearhead-integration, persistent-council, or
worker-tier actions. At most two replications may be proposed only if the first
screen is promising. A negative or stopped result completes the research
obligation honestly.

Cancel `coordination-dogfood` as an obsolete competing obligation. Its accepted
substrate findings remain intact, but its swarm framing and broad coordination
dogfood no longer express the selected experiment. This cancellation does not
reopen the workstream, session, declaration, or structured-handoff entities
cancelled by the coordination selection.

Keep the `multi-agent-coordination` charter open only for its independently
selected residual work: `orientation-worktree-context` and
`coordination-authority-boundary`. Its original entity-heavy walking-skeleton
and completion criteria are historical hypotheses rather than current build
commitments. Close or archive that charter only after the residual actions are
dispositioned.

## Why

The council review found the prose rules worth testing but supplied no evidence
for new machinery. The later plural-subject framing also removes the conceptual
basis for a supervisor or privileged council maintaining one total plan. A
research charter makes the uncertainty and stopping rule durable without
turning executions into actions or treating an attractive architecture as
already validated.

Sequencing behind knowledge-continuity dogfood keeps the collective's basic
inheritance substrate ahead of governance experimentation and prevents the new
idea from derailing the already-selected proof.

## Consequences

- Clearhead records the experiment as a project commitment but remains optional
  to the runtime protocol.
- The next plural-agency action is not ready until `knowledge-pulse-dogfood`
  completes.
- The owner must still choose the real question, authority assignments, spending
  ceiling, scorer, publication reviewer, and hard-stop checker before execution.
- Strong-council versus cheaper-worker economics remains outside the graph.
- Repeated concrete friction, not conceptual appeal, is required before any
  machinery proposal.
